One app for the trips you took and the ones you plan.

Hand-drawn world map

Visited countries are colored with your accent color. Switch between country and city views, with smooth zoom and a home marker.

Travel diary & planner

Log every stop with dates and means of transport. Upcoming trips get their own outline, past trips are grouped by year, and each trip can hold notes.

World statistics

Countries, capitals, continents, days on the road and your favorite way to travel, with a dedicated map for each continent.

Favorite cities

Build a bucket list of the places you love or dream of visiting, and turn any of them into a trip with one tap.

Share & backup

Create images of your stats for social media, share a single trip with a friend, and back up everything to a JSON file.

Your language, your names

English, Italiano and Deutsch, with city, region and country names translated automatically.

Questions before you download

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start marking your trips.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Planis does not collect or share data with third parties.

How do I keep my trips if I change phone?

Export all your trips to a JSON file from Settings and import it on your new device.

Which languages are supported?

English, Italiano and Deutsch. City, region and country names are translated too.

A city or place is missing. What can I do?

Use “Report a missing place” in Settings. It opens your email app with the details prefilled.
