Questions before you download

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start marking your trips.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Planis does not collect or share data with third parties.

How do I keep my trips if I change phone?

Export all your trips to a JSON file from Settings and import it on your new device.

Which languages are supported?

English, Italiano and Deutsch. City, region and country names are translated too.

A city or place is missing. What can I do?

Use “Report a missing place” in Settings. It opens your email app with the details prefilled.
